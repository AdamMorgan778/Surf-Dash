# Play

Ride the waves, launch off the crests and race to the finish line.

<div id="jetski-game" data-root="../" data-kicker="Arcade">The game needs JavaScript. Loading…</div>

<div class="jetski-help" markdown="1">

**Controls.** Hold the **right arrow** or **D** to throttle and the **left arrow** or **A** to brake. In the air, **up** and **down** (or **W** and **S**) tip the nose. **P** pauses and **R** restarts. On a phone, use the buttons under the game.

**Scoring.** Land level with the wave for a clean landing (+100, and a little speed). A rough landing scores less, and a bad one is a crash that slows you down. Time in the air and a quick finish add points. Your total is then **multiplied by this site's documentation coverage**, the same percentage as the score on the [Overview](../index.md), so the better documented the model, the more you score. [See what is missing](../documentation/index.md) to raise it.
